# Client checklist (for spa owners)

Send this to the spa owner once they say yes. It is plain-language and contains no
developer jargon or credentials. Missing assets are the number-one cause of delay,
so say so up front.

## What we need from you

**1. Your details**

- Spa or studio name (exactly as it should appear)
- A short tagline (one line, e.g. "Massage, facials and body rituals in Colombo 07")
- WhatsApp number and phone number
- Address, plus a Google Maps pin of your location
- Opening hours (note any closed days)
- Facebook / Instagram links (optional)

**2. Your treatments**

- A list of your treatments, grouped the way you'd like customers to see them
- For each: name, a one-line description, the **duration** (minutes) and the
  **price** (Rs.)
- Which ones you'd like to feature at the top

**3. Packages**

- Any bundled packages: name, what's included, total duration and price

**4. Photos**

- Up to 5 photos to start (your space, treatments, details). Clear, natural light,
  no text or logos. We optimise and crop them.

**5. Your domain**

- The domain you want (e.g. `yours pa.lk`) — or ask us to suggest one.

**6. Google Business Profile**

- If you already have one, give us access so we can update it. If not, we'll create
  and set it up for you.

## After you send everything

We build from the spa template, apply your brand, and show you a preview link. You
get one round of changes, then we go live and connect your domain. Prices and
availability are yours to update any time in a simple Google Sheet.

## How bookings work

There is no online payment and no booking calendar in the baseline. Customers tap
**Book on WhatsApp** and send you a pre-filled message with the treatment and their
preferred time. You confirm in chat. (A full booking system is available as an
add-on.)
